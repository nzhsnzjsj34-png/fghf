SURVIVAL BRIEFING — CLASSIFICATION: BLACK

Field Manual

01 · Light is a weapon Your lamp cone scorches Lurkers continuously — but the pain drives them into a charging frenzy, so the beam is a trade, not a free win. Aim with the mouse: the cone is where you look.
02 · Noise is a liability Every shot broadcasts your position and wakes creatures across the sector. Burst fire, then relocate. Flares are louder still — but they stun. Press T to kill the lamp and run dark: nothing tracks you, nothing burns, and you can barely see.
03 · Eyes in the dark Silhouettes are nearly invisible. Glowing eye pairs are not. Count them, then decide whether you have the magazine for it.
04 · Cores open the lift Clear a wave to make Signal Cores drop. Collect them all, then stand in the lift beam at the top of the sector to descend. Between sectors you install one salvage module.
05 · Threat dossier Lurker — stalks slowly in the dark, burns in your beam and frenzies when lit. Skitterling — fast pack hunter, telegraphs a lunge. Screamer — static, emits shock rings that damage and alert the sector. Broodmother — charges and splits when it dies. Rift Stalker — cloaks at range, telegraphs a pounce, and recoils from your lamp.
06 · Chain kills Kills inside a 3.4s window raise your multiplier up to ×25. Kill streaks call out triples through annihilations. Keep the chain alive for more salvage.
07 · Every deck is different Steam vents, spore pools, live busbars, and hydraulic presses vary by biome. A random sector protocol changes the rules; elite hostiles carry readable affixes. Use them against each other.
08 · Secondary weapon modes Hold RMB or G: the SMG channels an energy beam, the Breach-12 seeds proximity mines, and the Arc Lance raises an orbiting Aegis shield. Flares remain on E.
09 · Permanent salvage Bank salvage after every run and spend it in the HADAL Armory. Plating, damage, lamp, dash, flare, loader and even a one-use defibrillator carry between descents. Sector 10 contains the Warden of the Core.
10 · Sector contracts Each sector assigns a randomized optional contract: fight in darkness, dash often, avoid damage, clear quickly, or hunt a marked elite/Rift Stalker. The HUD tracks progress. Reach the lift to bank its salvage and score bonus.
