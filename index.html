SURVIVAL BRIEFING — CLASSIFICATION: BLACK

Field Manual

01 · Light is a weapon Your lamp cone scorches Lurkers continuously — but the pain drives them into a charging frenzy, so the beam is a trade, not a free win. Aim with the mouse: the cone is where you look.
02 · Noise is a liability Every shot broadcasts your position and wakes creatures across the sector. Burst fire, then relocate. Flares are louder still — but they stun. Press T to kill the lamp and run dark: nothing tracks you, nothing burns, and you can barely see.
03 · Eyes in the dark Silhouettes are nearly invisible. Glowing eye pairs are not. Count them, then decide whether you have the magazine for it.
04 · Cores open the lift Clear a wave to make Signal Cores drop. Collect them all, then stand in the lift beam at the top of the sector to descend. Between sectors you install one salvage module.
05 · Threat dossier Lurker — stalks slowly in the dark, burns in your beam and frenzies when lit. Skitterling — fast pack hunter, telegraphs a lunge. Screamer — static, emits shock rings that damage and alert the sector. Broodmother — charges, and splits into a litter when it dies.
06 · Chain kills Kills inside a 3.4s window raise your multiplier up to ×25. Dash through a lunge, flare a pack, and the score compounds fast.
